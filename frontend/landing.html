How PNG5 Governed IDE Works

From local workspace selection to instant verified deployment in four streamlined steps.

STEP 01

Open Local Workspace

Connect any folder on your computer. The IDE automatically indexes source files, dependencies, and git branches.

STEP 02

Instruct the AI to refactor code, redesign UI components, explain complex architectures, or fix failing tests.

Middleware classifies intent. Routine changes are auto-executed; sensitive operations trigger operator confirmation.
